About Discovered by Heart

What this search includes

The search looks for single ventricle terminology and common diagnoses, including hypoplastic left heart syndrome (HLHS), tricuspid atresia, double inlet left ventricle, mitral atresia, unbalanced atrioventricular canal defects, and pulmonary atresia with intact ventricular septum. It also looks for studies described by the Norwood, Glenn, and Fontan pathways.

Only studies listed as Recruiting or Not yet recruiting are shown.

Discovered by Heart is a Sisters by Heart resource for the single ventricle community.
